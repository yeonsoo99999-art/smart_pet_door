# Sound credits

One voice per cat personality. The difficulty mode picks the file, so the pitch
alone tells a visitor which cat they are talking to. All sources are free of
restrictions (CC0 or public domain); no attribution is required and this note
only records provenance.

| Mode | File | Length | Pitch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kitten | `meow_kitten.wav` | 0.81 s | ~1960 Hz, rising | *Cat Purr & Meow* by Kerzoven (`cat_softmew.wav`), CC0 — https://opengameart.org/content/cat-purr-meow |
| Adult | `meow_adult.wav` | 0.62 s | ~610 Hz, asking | *NoTrip* by Heismark, public domain — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NoTrip.ogg |
| Grumpy | `meow_grumpy.wav` | 0.85 s | ~245 Hz, demanding | *Meow of a Siamese cat* by freemaster2, CC0 — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_Siamese_cat_-_freemaster2.wav |

The adult clip is cut from a recording of a cat asking to be let outside, which is
exactly what this door is about. Only the isolated cat vocalisation is used; the
human speech elsewhere in that recording is not included.

Edits: trimmed to the mew itself, DC removed, peak normalised to −1 dBFS, 5 ms
fade-in and 50 ms fade-out. Format: WAV, mono, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz.
